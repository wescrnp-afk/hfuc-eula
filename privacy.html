Hamilton UC Finance — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 8, 2026

1. Overview

Hamilton UC Finance is a private internal application developed by and for Hamilton Family Urgent Care, Inc. ("Company"). This policy describes how the application handles data.

2. Data Collected

This application accesses QuickBooks Online accounting data including financial reports (Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow) for the Company's own QuickBooks account.

3. How Data Is Used

Data is used solely for generating internal financial reports for the Company. Reports are stored locally on Company-owned devices.

4. Data Sharing

No data is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third parties. All data remains within the Company's internal systems.

5. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored on secure, Company-controlled devices. Access is restricted to authorized personnel only.

6. Contact

Hamilton Family Urgent Care, Inc.
For privacy inquiries, contact the Company directly.
